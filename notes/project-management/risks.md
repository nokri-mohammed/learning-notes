**Core:** Catch problems early with a few simple records: a risk register, a quality register and a lessons log. Each item has an owner.

## Risk

- **Problem:** things can go wrong, and surprises cost more the later they appear.
- **Solution:** a risk register, ranked and owned, with a response for each risk.

- Risk: might happen (good or bad). Issue: has happened.
- Rate each risk by probability times impact. Handle the highest first.
- The register holds: ID, description, probability and impact, response, owner, status. It is a living document.
- Five responses:
  - Avoid: change the plan so the risk cannot happen.
  - Mitigate: reduce the chance or the impact.
  - Transfer: give it to someone else (insurance, a supplier).
  - Accept: do nothing, and keep watching.
  - Share: split it with a partner.
- Risk is highest at the start, when least is known.

## Quality

- **Problem:** a product that is on time and on budget but does not meet the need is still a failure.
- **Solution:** plan, assure and control quality.

- Quality means meeting the agreed requirements.
- Planning sets the standards. Assurance checks the process works. Control checks the product itself.
- The project manager owns both the quality of the management and the quality of the outputs.
- Faults found late cost much more than faults found early.

## Learning from experience

- A lessons log records what went well and what did not: event, effect, cause, recommendation, owner.
- Start it at project start by reading past projects. Share it, or it is useless.

## Health and safety

- Risk to life comes first. Employers owe a duty of care and are liable if they knew, or should have known, and did nothing.
- Never use cost to decide whether to deal with a risk to life.

## Configuration management

- **Problem:** several people edit the same documents, and nobody knows which version is current.
- **Solution:** version control: one agreed location, one clear latest version, changes only with approval, each change recorded with date and reason.
