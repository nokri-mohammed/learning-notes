# Project management on one page

## Organisation (shared by every project)
### Shareholders
- Own the company, elect the board
### Board of directors
- Governs: approves strategy, big investments, risk appetite
- Tool: governance (rules, roles, accountability)
### Executive team
- Runs the business, writes the strategy and business plan
- Tool: strategy, from vision to business plan, checked with PEST and SWOT
- Tool: business case, with NPV, IRR, payback and ROI
- Stakeholders are consulted and sign off
### Programme level
- Picks the right projects and groups them into programmes
- Tracks benefits
- Tool: maturity ladder, from ad hoc to optimised
### Support office (PPSO)
- Reports to the board
- Standards, templates, lessons, benefits tracking

## Per project
### Project board
- Executive: owns the business case, final say
- Senior User and Senior Supplier
- Authorises each stage, not a democracy
- Tool: PRINCE2 and its seven principles
- Supported by independent assurance and a change authority
### Project manager
- Runs the project day to day inside tolerances
- Tools: PBS, WBS, product flow, CPM, PERT, Gantt, life cycle
- Supported by stakeholder engagement and RACI
### Team manager
- Runs one team, delivers work packages
- Reports to the project manager
### Project team
- Does the work
- Agile is how the team delivers: small, frequent releases

## The journey
### Start
- Need or idea
- Needs analysis: the gap between now and wanted
- Project mandate
- Business case and appraisal
- Authorise: the board says go
### Run
- Plan: scope, breakdown, estimates, budget, schedule
- Do and control: work packages, change requests
- End of stage: the board continues or stops
### Finish
- Close and hand over to business as usual
- Benefits tracked, lessons logged

## Protections (every step)
### Risk
- Probability times impact, five responses, an owner
### Quality
- Plan, assure, control
### Health and safety
- Duty of care, risk to life first
### Change and configuration
- Change request, version control
### People and change
- Resistance comes from threat, so involve people
### Lessons learned
- Log it, read it at the start, share it
